# Customer-Superstore-Sales-Analysis-Dashboard-(Tableau)-
An interactive Tableau dashboard that analyzes sales, profit, and customer performance across US states, cities, months, and customer segments. It helps business users quickly see where money is made, where it is lost, and when sales peak.
# Image

# 🎯 Project Objective

-To build a single-page dashboard that answers key business questions for the sales and management team:

-What are the total sales, profit, orders, and profit margin?
-Which states generate the highest sales?
-Which months have the highest and lowest sales?
-Which customer segment brings the most profit?
-Which states are profitable and which are making a loss?
- Which cities contribute the most sales?

# 🗂️ Dataset
- Source: Sample Superstore dataset (Tableau sample data)
Size: ~10,000 rows of order-level data (5,009 unique orders)
Key fields: Order Date, State, City, Segment, Sales, Profit, Quantity, Discount

# 💡 Key Insights
California is the top state with 457,688 in sales, followed by New York (310,876) and Pennsylvania (116,512).
Sales are highest in November, December and September, showing strong year-end / festive season demand. February has the lowest sales.
The Consumer segment gives the highest profit (134,119), followed by Corporate (91,979) and Home Office (60,299).
Some states such as Pennsylvania have high sales but negative profit, which suggests heavy discounts or high costs there.
A small number of cities contribute most of the sales — the top city alone brings in 256,368.
Overall profit margin is 12.47%, so there is room to improve profitability.

# ✅ Business Recommendations
Plan stock and marketing campaigns before September–December to use the peak season.
Review discount policy in loss-making states to reduce losses.
Focus on the Consumer segment with loyalty offers, since it is the most profitable.
Run promotions in low-sales months (like February) to balance revenue across the year.
Expand in high-performing cities and states like California and New York.
